CITY OF CAMBRIDGE, MASSACHUSETTS
CAMBRIDGE TENANT RELOCATION ASSISTANCE ORDINANCE
Chapter 8.99 of the Municipal Code

Section 8.99.010 Short title and purpose.
This chapter may be cited as the Cambridge Tenant Relocation Assistance Ordinance. The City Council finds that tenants who lose their homes through no fault of their own face high moving costs and a tight rental market.

Section 8.99.020 Applicability.
This chapter applies to every residential building in the City of Cambridge that contains six or more dwelling units. This chapter does not apply to buildings owned by the City of Cambridge or by the Cambridge Housing Authority.

Section 8.99.030 Relocation payment.
When a landlord terminates a tenancy for any reason other than nonpayment of rent, the landlord shall pay each displaced tenant household a relocation payment equal to two months of rent within 10 days after the notice is delivered.

Section 8.99.040 Notice of termination.
A landlord must give a tenant written notice at least 90 days before the date on which the landlord will evict the tenant or otherwise end the tenancy under Section 8.99.030.

Section 8.99.050 Return of security deposit.
A landlord shall return the full security deposit of a displaced tenant household within 10 days after the tenant vacates the unit, and may not deduct the cost of ordinary wear and tear.

Section 8.99.060 Enforcement.
A landlord who violates this chapter shall be subject to a civil penalty of not more than 300 dollars for each day of violation.

Section 8.99.070 Effective date.
This ordinance shall take effect on January 1, 2027.
