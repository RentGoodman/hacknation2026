AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CHAPTER 218 (MULTIPLE DWELLINGS) SECTION 12
(PREVENTING ALGORITHMIC RENT-FIXING IN THE RENTAL HOUSING MARKET), OF THE
CODE OF ORDINANCES OF THE CITY OF JERSEY CITY.


COUNCIL AS A WHOLE offered and moved adoption of the following ordinance:

WHEREAS; the Municipal Council passed ordinance Ord. 25-057 at the May 21st 2025, Council meeting; and

WHEREAS, the Council wishes to clarify the language to ensure the ordinance is as strong as possible.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF JERSEY CITY
THAT: that Chapter 218 (Fees and Charges) of the Municipal Code shall be amended:
§218-12 Preventing Algorithmic Rent Fixing in the Rental Housing Market:

   1. No Change

   2. Unlawful Conduct

   1. It is unlawful for any real estate lessor, agent, or subcontractor thereof, to subscribe to, contract with, or
      otherwise exchange anything of value in return for or otherwise utilize the services of a Service
      Provider.

    2. No service provider shall facilitate an agreement not to compete among real estate lessors with respect to
       any aspect of residential dwelling units, including rental pricing, fees, or any other rental terms.
